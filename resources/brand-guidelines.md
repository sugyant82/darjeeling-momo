# Darjeeling Momo — brand basics (web & app)

Design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/JKqeA95KCeW4ypaEkeARQw

## Logo
- Chosen design: "Prayer Flags" (Logo C), recoloured with the Kanchenjunga Dawn palette and given the momo-and-peaks mark.
- Parts: a string of prayer flags in the five brand colours, the mark (a marigold momo in front of snow-capped indigo peaks, with steam), and the wordmark "DARJEELING" over "momo" in Baloo 2 ExtraBold.
- Two versions: light (transparent background) and navy (indigo panel). At very small icon sizes, use the momo alone.
- Tagline in use: "Himalayan street dumplings · South Auckland". The name is a working name, not final.
